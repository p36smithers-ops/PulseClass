Device setup help
WHOOP: In the WHOOP app open Device Settings → Status → enable Heart Rate Broadcast. Keep the WHOOP device worn and nearby.

Garmin watch: Some models can broadcast a standard Bluetooth heart-rate signal. Enable Broadcast Heart Rate before pairing, if your model supports Bluetooth broadcasting. The Garmin vívoactive 4 broadcasts over ANT+ rather than standard Bluetooth heart rate, so it will not connect directly to this browser.

Chest straps: Wet the electrode pads if required, wear the strap snugly, and wake it up before selecting it in the browser.

Browser: Students can join the lesson in a browser on phones, tablets, laptops and desktops. For live sensor pairing, use Chrome or Edge on Android, or a supported Chromium browser on a computer, over HTTPS. iPhone and iPad browsers do not currently provide the Bluetooth feature PulseClass needs, so those students can still join but cannot pair a sensor directly from the browser.
